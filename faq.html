Frequently Asked Questions

Buying a classic vehicle thousands of miles away can feel complicated. That’s why we’re here. American Classic Export gives you a trusted representative here in the United States to help you through the process from finding the right vehicle to getting it on its way to you.

1. How do I know the vehicle is really for sale and the seller is legitimate?

Before you purchase a vehicle, we communicate directly with the seller and work to verify the vehicle, seller, VIN and title information. We can request additional photos and videos and ask specific questions about the vehicle on your behalf.

Having someone here in the United States communicating directly with the seller adds an important layer of security when purchasing a vehicle from overseas. If something doesn’t look right, we’ll tell you.

2. How do I know what condition the vehicle is really in?

We want you to have as much information as reasonably possible before deciding to buy. We communicate directly with the seller and can request additional photographs, videos and answers to specific questions.

Depending on the vehicle, we may request videos showing a cold start, engine operation, gauges, lights, driving and other functions. We can also request detailed photographs of common problem areas such as the frame, floors, trunk, body panels and areas where rust or previous repairs may be present.

However, classic vehicles are used vehicles—often many decades old—and photographs, videos and seller descriptions cannot reveal every possible defect. We help you gather and evaluate information, but we cannot guarantee the mechanical or cosmetic condition of a vehicle.

3. Can you personally inspect the vehicle for me?

Sometimes. We are located near Chicago, Illinois, and personal inspections may be available for vehicles within a reasonable distance for an additional fee.

For vehicles located elsewhere in the United States, we may be able to help arrange an independent third-party inspection. Inspection availability and cost depend on the vehicle’s location.

4. Can you find a very specific vehicle?

Absolutely. That’s what our Car Finder service is designed to do.

Tell us the year, make, model, body style, engine, transmission, condition, budget and any other preferences you have. Whether you’re looking for a particular muscle car, pickup truck, conversion van, luxury car or unusual American classic, we’ll search the U.S. market for vehicles that match your requirements.

The more information you give us about your dream car, the better we can focus the search.

5. What happens when you find a vehicle I’m interested in?

We contact the seller and gather the information you need to decide whether the vehicle is right for you. We can ask questions, request additional photographs and videos and discuss the vehicle with the seller.

If you decide you want to purchase it, we’ll review the VIN and title information, negotiate with the seller, coordinate the purchase and arrange payment.

Once the purchase is complete, we arrange transportation to the U.S. shipping facility and help coordinate the paperwork needed on the U.S. side for export.

6. How do I safely pay for my vehicle?

American Classic Export provides you with a U.S.-based representative throughout the transaction.

Once you approve a purchase, you will receive payment instructions from American Classics Export Services LLC. We then coordinate payment to the U.S. seller as part of the purchasing process.

This also helps American sellers who may be uncomfortable accepting international payments or dealing directly with an overseas buyer.

For your security, always confirm payment instructions directly with us before sending funds. We will never change bank wiring instructions solely through an unexpected email or message without direct confirmation.

7. Whose name goes on the title and bill of sale?

Our role is to help you locate and purchase the vehicle”we are not purchasing the vehicle for our own inventory and reselling it to you. The title and bill of sale will be in your name as the buyer.

The appropriate buyer information is provided for the title and purchase documents based on the transaction. We will explain the paperwork process before you send payment so you understand how the vehicle will be documented.

8. Can you help if I’ve already found and purchased a car myself?

Yes. If you find a vehicle yourself, make the deal directly with the seller, and pay the seller directly, we can take over from there.

Our $495 Transport & Export Assistance service includes coordinating transportation of your vehicle to the U.S. shipping facility and assisting with the U.S.-side export paperwork.

If you would like us to contact the seller, ask questions, request additional photos or videos, negotiate the purchase price, coordinate the purchase, or handle payment to the seller, our $995 Full Vehicle Sourcing & Purchase Service applies.

You don’t have to use our search service to qualify for the full-service package. If you’ve already found the car you want but would like a trusted American representative to handle the seller and purchase process, we’re happy to step in and help.

9. What does the $995 Car Finder service include?

Our full Car Finder service includes:

  • Nationwide vehicle searching
  • Communication with U.S. sellers
  • Additional questions, photos and video requests
  • Purchase-price negotiation
  • Purchase coordination
  • Review of VIN and title information for the transaction
  • Coordination of U.S.-side export paperwork

A $150 prepaid search fee starts your nationwide search. If you purchase a vehicle through our Car Finder service, the full $150 is credited toward the $995 service fee.

Vehicle purchase price, inspections, ground transportation, international shipping, taxes, duties and other third-party costs are separate unless specifically stated otherwise.

10. How does transportation work?

Once your vehicle is purchased, we can arrange transportation from the seller’s location to the U.S. shipping facility that will ship to your preferred port.

We work to find competitive transportation options and coordinate directly with the seller and transporter for pickup.

Our transportation coordination fee is $195. The actual cost of transporting the vehicle is separate and depends on distance, vehicle size, location, accessibility and current transportation rates.

11. How long will it take for my vehicle to arrive?

Many vehicles may reach their European destination in approximately 12 weeks after purchase, but every shipment is different.

Timing depends on several factors, including the vehicle’s location in the United States, availability of ground transportation, shipping schedules, port operations, destination country and customs processing.

We’ll help keep you informed as your vehicle moves through the U.S. portion of the process, but international shipping and arrival dates cannot be guaranteed.

12. Do you work with European classic-car dealers?

Yes. We welcome relationships with established European dealers looking for a reliable partner in the United States.

We can act as your U.S. contact for locating vehicles, communicating with sellers, negotiating purchases, coordinating payments and arranging transportation to the shipping facility.

We also offer a buyer-first sourcing model that can reduce the need to invest in speculative inventory. We can identify promising vehicles available in the United States that you can market to your customers before committing to purchase them.

Visit our For Dealers page or contact us to discuss a partnership.
